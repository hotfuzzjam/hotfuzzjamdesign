Connect the truck

  1. Plug a Bluetooth LE OBD-II adapter (OBDLink CX or vLinker FS) into the diagnostic port under the dash on the driver's side.
  2. Turn the ignition on.
  3. Open this page in Bluefy on the iPad. Safari and Chrome on iPad can't talk to Bluetooth devices.
  4. Tap OBD at the top and pick the adapter. Don't pair it in iPad Settings first.
