Questions

Before you load in.

Still unsure? Email hello@hotfuzzjam.com.au and we'll get back to you.

Do I need to bring drums?

The kick, rack tom and floor tom are supplied. Bring your snare, cymbals and hardware.

Is there a guitar amp?

Cabs only: two 4×12s, a 2×12 on request and a 4×10 bass cab. Bring your head or combo.

Can I practise solo?

Yes. Book any hour at the standard rate.

How do I get in?

Your door code arrives in the booking confirmation email.

Can I cancel?

Free up to 24 hours before your session, from the link in your confirmation email. Later than that, you can still cancel but the session isn’t refunded.

Where do I park and load in?

Parking right out the front, and a trolley inside to bring in your gear.
